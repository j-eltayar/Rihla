**Last Updated:** January 22, 2026

## Introduction

"For Rania 💖" is a personal mobile application designed for private use. This privacy policy outlines how the app handles information.

## Information Collection and Use

### Personal Information
This app does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers or third parties. The app is designed as a private, personal application.

### Local Data Storage
- The app may store preferences and settings locally on your device
- All data remains on your device and is not shared with any external services
- No user accounts or authentication are required (except for password-protected sections within the app)

### Music Playback
- The app plays audio files that are bundled within the application
- No music streaming or external audio services are used
- No listening history or usage data is collected

### Emergency Call Feature
- The app includes an emergency support feature that allows users to make phone calls
- This feature uses your device's native phone calling functionality
- No call data or logs are stored or transmitted by the app

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Collect any personal data
- Share any information with third parties
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Display advertisements
- Require internet connectivity for core functionality

## Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

- **Audio Playback:** To play music within the app

## Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
